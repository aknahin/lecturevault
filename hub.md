# LectureVault

A plain-Markdown Obsidian study vault for students: 18 files (17 Markdown
notes plus LICENSE.txt), no code, no scripts, no CSV. It structures a semester
of lectures — Cornell and outline lecture-note templates held to a 4-key
front-matter contract (course, date, lecturer, topics), a per-course map of
content, a semester planner, exam-countdown and semester-overview dashboards
(one filled Fall 2026 example plus a blank shell), a 1/3/7/14/30-day
spaced-repetition revision ladder, a study-session log, a running-grade tracker
that excludes unscored rows instead of scoring them zero, an FC-## flashcard
inbox, an SQ3R note, a study-methods guide, and two reconciled fictional
worked examples.

**Honest note:** this is study structure, not a guarantee of any grade. It
organizes how you review and track a semester; what you learn is still on you.
